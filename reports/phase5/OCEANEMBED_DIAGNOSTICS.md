## 1. Executive Diagnostic Synthesis

Our empirical diagnostics on the training (Jan–Jul 2020) and validation (Aug 2020) partitions have uncovered the exact mathematical and architectural mechanisms behind OceanEmbedNet's underperformance:

1. **Severe Thermocline Error Concentration**:
   - The depth window of **$75\text{m}–150\text{m}$ accounts for exactly $66.5\%$ of the entire validation Mean Squared Error (MSE)**.
   - At $125\text{m}$, OceanEmbed's validation RMSE is **$3.53^\circ\text{C}$**, compared to **$1.31^\circ\text{C}$ for SimpleCNN** ($2.68\times$ higher error).
2. **Variance Collapse & Extreme Over-Smoothing in the Pycnocline**:
   - At $100\text{m}$ and $125\text{m}$, the true spatial temperature standard deviation is $\sigma = 2.47^\circ\text{C}–2.59^\circ\text{C}$.
   - OceanEmbed's predictions exhibit a standard deviation of only $\sigma = 1.06^\circ\text{C}–1.22^\circ\text{C}$ (**Variance Ratio = $0.18–0.22$**). OceanEmbed reproduces only $\sim 20\%$ of the real physical spatial thermal variance, collapsing toward an over-smoothed profile.
   - The model develops a massive cold bias in the thermocline ($-2.98^\circ\text{C}$ at $125\text{m}$) and a compensatory warm bias near the surface ($+1.19^\circ\text{C}$ at $5\text{m}$).
3. **The Spatial Bottleneck Paradox**:
   - SimpleCNN operates at **full horizontal resolution ($101 \times 241$) across all layers with zero downsampling**.
   - OceanEmbed downsamples spatial features by $8\times$ ($101 \times 241 \to 12 \times 30$ at the bottleneck). Mesoscale thermal fronts and sharp eddy boundaries are smoothed out at the bottleneck.
4. **Decoder Weight Sharing & Vanishing Depth Gradients**:
   - OceanEmbed forces a **single shared decoder (`dec3`, `dec2`, `dec1`, `final_conv`) to reconstruct all 15 depths**, with depth identity supplied solely as a 128-dimensional embedding vector concatenated at the coarse $12 \times 30$ bottleneck.
   - The gradient norm of the `depth_embedding` is only **$0.0316$**, the weakest of any module in the network (compared to $0.4973$ in `final_conv` and $0.2839$ in `enc1`).
   - The surface skip connections (`e1`, `e2`, `e3`), which carry zero depth awareness, overpower the weak depth embedding in the decoder, forcing the shared filters into a blurry compromise across depths.
5. **Severe Generalization Gap on Training vs Validation**:
   - Training loss dropped to **$0.1463$**, while validation loss plateaued at **$2.8011$** (gap of $2.7753$). OceanEmbed overfits to the summer training distribution while failing to generalize across seasonal pycnocline shifts.

- **Finding**: Gradients are heavily concentrated in `final_conv` and `up1_dec1`. Backpropagated depth gradients decay severely by the time they reach the latent bottleneck and `depth_embedding`.

### 6, 7, 8 & 9. Prediction Variance, Bias, and Mean by Depth
- **Surface (0–20m)**: Warm bias of $+0.84^\circ\text{C}$ to $+1.19^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Thermocline (75–150m)**: Severe cold bias peaking at **$-2.98^\circ\text{C}$ at 125m** and **$-2.68^\circ\text{C}$ at 100m**.
- **Variance Ratio**: Drops from $0.57$ at 50m to **$0.18$ at 150m** and **$0.19$ at 125m**. The model drastically suppresses spatial variability in the thermocline.

### 10. Latent Embedding Collapse Check
- Latent tensor dimensions: $[10, 128, 12, 30]$.
- Active channels: **128 / 128 (0 dead channels)**.
- Effective SVD rank: **87.0 out of 128**.
- **Finding**: The latent space itself has NOT collapsed to a trivial representation. The representations exist in the bottleneck, but the decoder fails to translate them into depth-stratified spatial variance.

### 11 & 12. Depth Decoder Bottleneck & Depth Conditioning
- In `OceanEmbedNet`, depth is only concatenated once at the $12 \times 30$ bottleneck.
- The decoder must reconstruct 15 distinct vertical regimes using identical weights.
- When `depth_indices` change, the network shifts its climatological prior and coarse background, but the high-frequency spatial structures passed via `e1` and `e2` skips are completely unconditioned by depth.

### 13. Depth Loss Dominance
- Total depth-averaged validation MSE: **$3.6324$**.
- Cumulative MSE of 75m, 100m, 125m, and 150m: **$2.4150$**.
- **Thermocline fraction: $66.5\%$ of all validation loss**.

### 14. Underfitting vs Overfitting Diagnosis
- Overfitting on the training set is pronounced (Train MSE = 0.146 vs Val MSE = 2.92).
- However, at the thermocline, even training RMSE is high ($3.25^\circ\text{C}$ at 125m on training data), indicating that the shared-decoder architecture fundamentally lacks the capacity to represent sharp vertical gradients.

### 15. Thermocline Gradient Conflicts
- When all 15 depths are decoded in the same pass and averaged into an unweighted loss, surface mixed layer errors (where $\sigma$ is small) pull decoder weights toward surface preservation, creating gradient interference with the sharp pycnocline layers.

## 3. The Core Question Answered

### "Why does a 46K-parameter SimpleCNN outperform a 1.34M-parameter OceanEmbed?"

| Dimension | SimpleCNNBaseline (46,031 Parameters) | OceanEmbedNet (1,342,928 Parameters) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spatial Resolution** | Preserves full $101 \times 241$ resolution across all 3 layers. Zero pooling. | Compresses resolution by $8\times$ ($101 \times 241 \to 12 \times 30$). Loses sharp fronts. |
| **Depth Projection** | Final 1x1 conv maps directly from 64 spatial features to **15 separate depth channels** simultaneously. | A single shared decoder loops 15 times, decoding each depth through the identical convolutional filters. |
| **Depth Specialization** | Each depth has its own dedicated 64 weights + bias, allowing independent vertical gradients. | Depths differ only by a 128-dim vector at the coarse bottleneck, diluted by depth-unaware skip connections. |
| **Receptive Field** | Local $5 \times 5$ effective receptive field ($3 \times 3 + 3 \times 3$), perfectly matched to local geostrophic SST/SSH anomalies. | Global multi-scale field ($>50 \times 50$) that over-regularizes and smooths out local vertical displacements. |
| **Thermocline RMSE (125m)** | **1.31°C** | **3.53°C** |

## 4. Concrete Action Plan for Phase 5B (Controlled Improvement Sprint)

Based on these empirical diagnostic findings, our improvement sprint will target:
1. **Experiment 1 (Depth-Balanced Loss)**: Apply moderate additional loss weighting to the 75–150m thermocline (which accounts for $66.5\%$ of loss) to force the optimizer to allocate capacity to steep vertical gradients.
2. **Experiment 2 (Improved Decoder / Direct Multi-Depth Projection)**:
   - Provide depth conditioning directly to decoder skip connections (FiLM modulation or depth-specific projection) so that high-resolution skip features are properly scaled per depth.
   - Alternatively, evaluate a multi-depth output head that directly projects latent features into all 15 depths simultaneously, eliminating the shared single-channel decoder bottleneck.
3. **Model Selection**: Strictly governed by August 2020 validation MSE.
